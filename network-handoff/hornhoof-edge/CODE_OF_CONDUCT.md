# Code of conduct

Hornhoof Edge is built by people from different countries, with different experience and different first languages. We want everyone here to feel welcome, whether they report their first bug or send their hundredth pull request.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and explain instead of judging.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Criticize code and ideas, never people.
- Plain words over clever ones: many of us read in a second language.
- Welcome newcomers and help them find their way.
- Accept a "no" on a change gracefully, and say "no" with a reason.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats, or jokes about anyone's identity, body or background.
- Sexual language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Pushing on after someone asked you to stop.

## Our full rules

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), in issues, pull requests and every other place where Hornhoof Edge's work happens.

## Reporting

If something here made you feel unwelcome or unsafe, tell the maintainers privately: open the repository's **Security** tab, click **Report a vulnerability** and say it is about conduct. Only the maintainers see it. We look into every report, keep the reporter's name to ourselves, and may warn, block or remove anyone who breaks these rules.
