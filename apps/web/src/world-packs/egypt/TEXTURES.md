# Ancient Egypt pack: ground, water and plants, sources and licences

Every picture of the map is **made by us**, and nothing comes from a commercial game.

## Painted art (what the map shows)

The ground, water, paths, small ground details and plants are painted art that was **generated by us for Hornhoof**
on 22 November 2026 and checked against the approved reference 01 (`01-ETALON-interfejs-i-stil.png`). It is ours to
use; no outside picture, photograph or game sprite was copied or traced. By the art contract
(`../../world/ARTIST_CONTRACT.md`) art files never go into this repository: they live in the pack's art folder,
served at `/world-art/egypt/` (locally `apps/web/public/world-art/egypt/`, ignored by git). The delivered sets are the
archives `egypt-ground-painted.tar.gz` and `egypt-veg-painted.tar.gz` attached to task #1914; each has its own
`README.md` with sizes, colour checks and the wind rules.

| Picture | Files in the art folder | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Ground textures, 1024 x 1024, 256 texels per tile, seamless | `ground/plain.png`, `cleared.png`, `dune.png`, `bank.png`, `fertile.png`, `ridge.png`, `quarry.png` | materials sand and dust, cleared (building lots, #1958), dune, bank and silt, grass, rock, quarry (`terrainArt.ground` in `pack.ts`) |
| Edge masks, 1024 x 256 grey | `ground/edges/plain_bank.png`, `bank_fertile.png`, `plain_dune.png`, `plain_ridge.png`, `river_bank.png` | painted edges between grounds and at the waterline (`terrainArt.edges`, `shoreEdge`) |
| Water layers | `ground/river_bed.png`, `river_ripples.png`, `river.png` | the animated water (bed relief and two drifting ripple layers over the base colour) and irrigation ditches |
| Path strip | `roads/trail.png` | worn paths over the sand (`terrainArt.pathStrip`) |
| Street strips, 1024 long, as tall as the street is wide | `roads/trail.png`, `dirt.png`, `brick.png`, `stone.png`, `avenue.png` | the city's streets by tier, baked into the ground, and the 2-tile avenue the busiest stretches widen into (`terrainArt.roads`, #1985, #2003) |
| Ground details | `decals/grass_tuft_*.png`, `pebbles_*.png`, `sherds_01.png`, `cracks_*.png` | flat tufts and pebbles (`meadow_tuft`, `sandstone` in `plants.ts`) |
| Plants with wind weight maps and passports | `veg/<name>.png`, `veg/<name>_w.png`, `veg/<name>.json`: date palms, doum palms, papyrus, reed, green and dry bushes, desert thorn, tamarisk, dry grass, acacia, sycamore | every plant kind in `plants.ts` (`art`) |

The delivered `ground/field.png` and `ground/edges/fertile_field.png` are not drawn: the sown fields left the map on Ivan's word, 23.11.2026 (`ART.md`, "Sown fields are not landscape"). The art check warns that the pack does not declare them.

## Procedural fallback (only while an art file is missing)

When the art folder is absent (tests, a fresh checkout) the engine draws stand-ins generated by code in this
repository, licensed like the repository (MIT):

| Picture | Where |
|---|---|
| Ground textures | `getMaterialTextureRgb` in `src/world/smooth-terrain-baker.ts`, materials in `pack.ts` |
| Water | `buildLiquidSurfacePixels` in `src/world/smooth-terrain-baker.ts`, `liquidLayer` in `pack.ts` |
| Plants | `src/world/plant-sprites.ts`, palettes in `plants.ts` |

Plant kinds are only named after real plants for readability.
