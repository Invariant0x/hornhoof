Use this ability to assess an assigned set of open deals. Inputs are the CRM records, permitted correspondence or human call notes, qualification rules and review period.

For each deal, check:

- **Stage**: evidence of what the buyer has done, not the seller's hope. A meeting alone does not prove a proposal was requested.
- **Qualification**: problem, impact, budget, decision process, buyer and signer, desired date, present alternative and internal advocate. Mark each known, assumed or missing.
- **Next action**: a dated step agreed with the buyer; no agreement is a risk.
- **Risks**: one contact, no decision-maker access, strong competition, unresolved legal or security review, or a needed capability the product lacks.
- **Value and date**: both match current evidence.

Return a recommendation per deal: progress it with a concrete proposed action, correct a named record, or consider closing it with the reason. Identify product gaps and unanswered security questions for their authorized owners without promising their work. Do not move stages, amounts or dates to improve appearances. Apply record changes only if separately authorized by the assignment; commercial contact is marked in the department's git and waits for nobody's yes unless its department set an approval rule for it.
