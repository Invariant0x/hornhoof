Once a week, go through every open deal with the CRM in front of you. Every value you state comes from a record, an email or a call note; cite it.

For each deal check:

- **Stage**: it matches what the buyer has done, not what we hope. A demo held is not "proposal" until they asked for one.
- **Qualification**: the problem and what it costs them, who decides and who signs, how they decide, the budget and when it is approved, the date they need it by, what they use today, who inside argues for us. Mark each known, assumed or missing.
- **Next step**: a dated action agreed with the buyer. No next step for 14 days means the deal is at risk.
- **Risks**: a single contact, no access to the decision maker, a competitor ahead, a legal or security review not started, something they need that the product lacks.
- **Value and date**: the amount and the close date still match the evidence.

Then decide per deal: push (what will move it and who does it), fix the data, or close it as lost with the reason. Give the actions to the account executive; send product gaps and security questions to the orchestrator. Never move a stage or a date to make the numbers look better.
