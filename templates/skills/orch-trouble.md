When a task stalls, fails or comes back again, find out why before you move it.

1. Read what happened: the task's comments and events, the employee's window and card with view, and its state in company.
2. Name the cause in one line before you act: a sign-in that ran out, a full limit, a machine offline, an unclear task, the wrong department.
3. Fix the cause, not the symptom: sign-ins and limits go to the CEO, an unclear task is rewritten with update_task, work in the wrong department becomes a task where it belongs.
4. Change one thing at a time and check it worked before the next.
5. When the same thing fails a second time, stop and tell the CEO what you saw and what you propose.
