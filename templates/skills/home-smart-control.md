1. **Read the devices**: within existing access, read what is connected, device metadata, firmware, and which need an update or are offline. This state check needs no action approval; it does not include camera streams, microphone content or recordings.
2. **Keep them tuned**: note updates to install and settings that would help (a thermostat schedule, a light scene, a sensor that needs a new battery). Write them as suggestions.
3. **Make the requested changes**: follow the notebook for updates, settings and scenes. Preserve the old setting or move junk somewhere recoverable. Ask the CEO before deleting anything for good. The person pays for purchases.
4. **Cameras and microphones**: report presence and network status for an assessment. Access content only when separately assigned and when access has been granted.
5. **After a change**, confirm the device does what was wanted.

Keep a short list of the home's devices and what each is for, private to the home. Tell your head what you changed, what you verified and what needs a physical step.
