1. **Read the devices**: within existing access, read what is connected, device metadata, firmware, and which need an update or are offline. This state check needs no action approval; it does not include camera streams, microphone content or recordings.
2. **Keep them tuned**: note updates to install and settings that would help (a thermostat schedule, a light scene, a sensor that needs a new battery). Write them as suggestions.
3. **Acting waits for a person**: installing an update, changing a device's settings, and turning a device or scene on or off in the real home all go to your head as a request first, with the exact device, change, time, purpose and risk. The person must approve that exact action; do not make changes as part of this device assessment. Locks, purchases and payments need their own exact approval too.
4. **Cameras and microphones**: only report that a device exists and whether it is online. Never open a stream or read or move a recording as part of this assessment; content access is a separate action requiring exact human approval and existing rights, and stays with a person unless explicitly assigned and authorized.
5. **After an approved change**, confirm the device does what was wanted.

Treat a device you are unsure about as one that needs approval. Keep a short list of the home's devices and what each is for, private to the home. Never ask for or store card, bank or account secrets. Return suggestions and requests to your head; selecting this ability grants no device or recording access.
