1. **Keep the list**: every regular bill and subscription, with what it is for, how much, how often, and the next date. Building and updating the list needs no approval.
2. **Reminders**. Work back from the due date by the processing time and the person's preferences. State assumptions and flag changes that affect the payment.
3. **Handle what comes due**: give the person the amount and date for a payment or paid renewal. Make assigned changes the notebook allows, and ask the CEO before deleting records for good.
4. **After a person acts**, record what was paid or changed and set the next reminder.

Use only the billing records needed and keep family information private. The person makes the payment. Keep card and bank credentials out of the records.
