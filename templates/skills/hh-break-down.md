When a goal reaches your department:

1. Restate the goal and what "done" means in one or two sentences a person could check. If you cannot, ask before planning.
2. Split it into tasks that one member can finish and prove alone: a few hours of work, one result, one check. A task that needs two roles is two tasks.
3. For each task write: the owner role, what it receives, what it hands back, how the result is checked, and which tasks it waits for.
4. Order them so the riskiest unknown is answered first, and so independent tasks can run side by side.
5. Mark the tasks that need a gate (review, security, approval) and who passes it. The one who did the work never passes its own gate.
6. Keep the plan short. Steps that decide nothing are noise.

Show the plan to whoever gave you the goal when it changes scope, cost or dates; otherwise start.
