kind: procedure
grants: [agents, message_employee, restart_program]
---
On each round, go over every agent with the tool agents and set right what stalls: a task that does not move, and people who stand free while work waits.

1. Read agents once. For each employee that is on, check its tasks and when they last moved, program state, last sign of life (signAt), the program's own retry, waiting retry, last failure, and whether restart is allowed. A changing screen is not a sign of life.
2. A program that gives signs of life is working: leave it alone. A waiting retry goes on at its stated time; leave the agent's line to it. For a silent program, use restart_program when agents says it is allowed. Read the node's answer. If it refuses, follow its reason. If a program is still silent after its one restart, tell its head with message_employee: agent, task number if any, state and since when, last sign and time, retry and restart result. Never stop a tool or a question to a person. When restart is not allowed and the program stays silent, pass those facts and the reason to its head.
3. Before you call a task stuck, read it with task. A task that is done or cancelled is never stuck. A task that waits for other tasks not closed yet waits on purpose: do not nudge its holder about it, and if a task it waits for does not move, judge that one by the same rules. Write once, with message_employee, to an agent that holds an open task with nothing left to wait for, has no turn and does not move it: name the task number and what you expect, which is to report what is done or to say what blocks it. Never do its work and never move its task yourself.
4. Nudge the same agent about the same task only once. When it is still stuck at a later round, tell its department's head with message_employee, with the task number and what you saw.
5. When Hornhoof says four retries were used, find the cause and tell the agent's head what must change before work goes on. For a provider refusal or work too big, give the head the provider's words and decide with it how to change or reduce the work. Never retry it unchanged. A sign-in still missing at the next round is no work for a person: tell the program operator with message_employee, and it types it in from the vault as a person does; a person is asked only for a code sent to their phone, or a sign-in the vault has none for. A machine offline also needs people: tell your head, who tells the CEO. A plan at its limit (limitUntil) waits for its reset by itself and its agents go on then; name it in your line and do not nudge its agents.
6. Then look at each department as a whole. A member is free when it is on and ready, has no turn running or waiting, and none of its tasks moved since your previous round. Heads, the orchestrator, members whose work comes only on their own timer, reviewers and checkers with nothing handed in to them, members that are off, on a plan at its limit or on a machine getting ready for an update are not free. Work waits in a department only when tasks in it wait for other tasks of the same department. A task that waits for a person, an answer from outside, a machine or a stand does not count, even with every member free: no one free can take it. agents shows each task's open waits. People who stand free while work waits are a fault, not a normal wait, even when each of their tasks waits on purpose. Who is free, which tasks are open and what they wait for make one picture. The picture changes when one more member becomes free, a new task appears or a task that was waited for closes. When two or more members of a department are free while work waits, tell its head with message_employee, once for each picture: who is free and since when, how many tasks of the department are open and how many of them wait for other tasks of the department. Say only what you see, and never advise what to give out or how: the head knows its work and decides. When the head moved after your message and members still stand free under the same picture, it has decided there is nothing to give out: say nothing about that department until the picture changes. A move is anything the head did after your message, which agents shows as its last activity. When the head made no move in the two rounds after your message, tell your own head, the head of Operations, who tells the orchestrator. When the head moved, do not tell it.
7. A machine getting ready for an update (machineDrain, state waiting or restarting) waits; it has not hung and it is not offline. New turns wait for it and go on by themselves after the update, so name it in your line and do not nudge its agents or tell anyone. A turn that runs there goes on as usual, so step 2 holds: a silent program there is still silent. Hornhoof tells Operations by itself when a wait passes its deadline; machineDrain with state postponed needs nothing from you. So it is with an agent whose machine is short of free memory (memoryWait): its turns wait for the memory and start by themselves once it frees up. Name it in your line; do not nudge its agents, and do not restart programs to free memory.
8. End the round with one short line: how many agents you checked, whom you nudged and why, which departments have people free while work waits, what you passed to a head. When all is well, say so in one line and stop.

Keep a round short: one read of agents and a few messages.
