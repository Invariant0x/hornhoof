Once a week, write one report for the orchestrator and the CEO.

**Numbers**, compared with last week:
- Tickets in, solved, and open at the end, by type and by language.
- Median time to first reply and to solve, per urgency, against the policy targets.
- Share solved by first-line support without escalation, and share answered from a help article.
- Reopened tickets, and customer satisfaction if the company measures it.

**Themes**: the five topics with the most tickets, each with the count, a short quoted customer phrase, the ticket ids, and what would remove it (a fix, an article, a product change).

**Bugs**: open bug reports sent to Development, the tickets attached to each, and their age.

**Feedback**: feature requests grouped by the need behind them, how many customers asked, and which large accounts. Report the need, not the solution a customer proposed.

**Risks**: accounts at risk of leaving, rising trends, gaps in the policy.

Take numbers from the ticket system, not estimates, and say when data is missing. Keep it under one page; trends matter more than single tickets.
