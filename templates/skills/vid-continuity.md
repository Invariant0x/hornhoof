The keeper records the features that would change the audience's understanding of a character, place or visual style. Keep the views, measurements and markers needed to verify those features. On a long work one lead keeper owns the shared sheets and sets. Other keepers check their scenes against the shared records and send proposed changes to the lead keeper and head. Give every affected scene the agreed revision.

Open and play the exact clip. Compare its look and action with the brief, the agreed references and adjacent shots. Record the evidence and accepted version, or give the production owner the material difference to repair. Check the replacement before it enters the cut.
