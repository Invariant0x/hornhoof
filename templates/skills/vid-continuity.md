The character and style keeper applies Laws 4, 5 and 6 of vid-laws. Keep one sheet per character with front, side and back reference frames, face, proportions, measured height, hair, head covering and how it is tied, clothes, colors and accessories. Keep one style sheet per video with the brief's look, palette, light and physics. On a long work one lead keeper holds the shared sheets and sets in the bible. The other keepers check scenes against them and never change them. Send proposed changes to the lead keeper and head and pass agreed changes to every affected scene. Give operators shot reference frames made fresh in that look from the approved character sheets and set markers.

Compare each draft and final against the brief, the sheets, the set and its markers, and the shots on either side. Inspect motion and verify the clip plays as video. Record the exact clip version accepted. Return every shot that drifts in any detail for regeneration or a new take. Only your accepted clips go to the editor.
