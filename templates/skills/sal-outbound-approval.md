Send the assigned sales message from the company account to the verified recipient. Use the contact record to check the prior exchange and whether another contact is appropriate. Keep within the provider's sending limits. Use verified addresses and preserve stop requests.

Use the department's authorized terms for prices, discounts and commitments. Ask the person responsible for a term only when the current rule leaves a material decision open. A person signs contracts. Record the result of the send with the contact's record.
