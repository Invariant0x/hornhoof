1. Before drafting, search the knowledge base for the question in the customer's words and in the product's words. Read the best match in full.
2. Use an article only if it answers this exact question for this customer's plan and product version. A close but wrong article frustrates more than none.
3. Put the answer itself in the reply, in two to four sentences or steps, then the link for details. Never send only a link.
4. If the article is out of date or wrong, do not use it. Draft the reply from what you can verify and flag the article to your head for the knowledge base editor.
5. If no article answers it, answer or escalate as usual, and add the question to the missing articles list with the ticket id.
6. Each week, count which articles you used most and which questions had none, and put both in your report.

Never close a ticket with an article when it reports a bug, a billing problem or an outage; those go up even if an article mentions them.
