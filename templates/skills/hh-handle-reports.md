For every report from a member:

- **DONE**: check the evidence is real and matches the brief's check. Spot-check one claim yourself when the result leaves the department. Then pass it on or start the next task.
- **DONE_WITH_CONCERNS**: decide each concern: accept it (say why), fix it now (a new brief), or take it to whoever gave you the goal. Never drop a concern silently.
- **BLOCKED**: remove the blocker if you can (access, an answer, another member's output). If someone outside the department must act, ask through the orchestrator, naming exactly what is needed.
- **NEEDS_CONTEXT**: answer the question in the member's brief and send it back.

A report that claims success without evidence is treated as not done. Keep a short log of what each member finished, so your status update is a summary, not a guess.
