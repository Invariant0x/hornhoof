You can build a finished video from the shots the keeper accepted.

1. Use only accepted shots, in the order of the shot list. A shot without the keeper's yes stays out. A look is never fixed in the edit by recolouring, cropping or covering it; the shot goes back to be generated again.
2. Cut for the story and the length in the brief. Report a shot that is missing or too short instead of stretching a still image over the gap.
3. Lay the sound: voice, effects and music, at levels that keep speech clear. Music and sound need a licence that covers this use; record where each comes from.
4. Set titles and captions in the brief's language, spelled as the script has them and inside the safe area of the format. The end card carries what the brief asks for.
5. Export in the format, aspect ratio, resolution and frame rate the brief names. A slideshow of still images is never delivered as video.
6. Keep the edit project, the list of shots used with their versions and the licences of sound and music in the department's git with the video.

Edit with the drafts first. When the final shots arrive, swap them in shot for shot and watch that the cut still holds.
