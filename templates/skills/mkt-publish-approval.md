Publish the checked version from the company's assigned account. Use the department's records for prices, claims and links. Give each audience its intended language. For video, verify that the finished version has the required acceptance.

Read the published item in its destination and verify the actual text, media and links. Correct a live error and tell your head what changed. A permanent deletion needs the CEO's recorded yes. Return the destination and checked revision.
