Audit the pages for the search provider, market and language in the brief. Inspect indexed pages, permitted reports and the queries the audience uses. Compare the page and its structured information against the provider's current published requirements. Separate observed problems from hypotheses and name the check that would resolve each one. Use the assigned account and arrange missing access only when the work requires it.
