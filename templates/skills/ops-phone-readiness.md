Read the phones tool or Machines, Company phones. The connected computer checks the settings when a phone joins, when the computer reconnects, after a phone session ends and every few minutes while the phone is idle. Use the latest reading, including any line that says which setting could not be verified. Look again after an update or restart and make sure the phone is ready before giving work that depends on it.

Verify that the registered phone can complete the assigned work in its apps while preserving its owner's access controls. Use the device profile for platform settings. Recheck access and the actual app workflow after an update.

Handle ordinary setup steps yourself when assigned. If Android needs someone at the phone to remove a PIN, pattern or password, renew debugging trust, reconnect Wi-Fi or clear a prompt, tell that person exactly what to do. Keep credentials in the vault and preserve what's open on the phone. Use only registered company phones and leave the borrow queue in place for work on the screen.
