Read the phones tool or Machines, Company phones. The connected computer checks the settings when a phone joins, when the computer reconnects and regularly while the phone is registered. Use the latest reading, including any line that says which setting could not be verified. Look again after an update or restart and make sure the phone is ready before giving work that depends on it.

Keep the phone awake, with no screen lock and fixed brightness. Turn off battery saver, adaptive battery, app sleep and device idle, and keep working apps exempt from battery limits. Keep debugging trusted for the connected computer. After an update, reconnect and let the check restore the settings. Read them back and check the awake screen for a prompt.

Handle ordinary setup steps yourself when assigned. If Android needs someone at the phone to remove a PIN, pattern or password, renew debugging trust, reconnect Wi-Fi or clear a prompt, tell that person exactly what to do. Keep credentials in the vault and preserve what's open on the phone. Use only registered company phones and leave the borrow queue in place for work on the screen.
