1. **Read the state**: which devices are online, signal strength by room, the internet connection, firmware versions, and any outage or slowdown. Reading needs no approval.
2. **Find the weak spots**: a room with poor coverage, an old firmware, a channel that clashes with neighbours, a device nobody recognises on the network.
3. **Propose, do not change**: write the change in plain words, what it fixes, and any risk (a short restart, a device dropping off). Changing a router's or the network's settings waits for a person; hand it to your head as a request.
4. **After an approved change**, check that the home is back to normal and say what changed.
5. **Keep a short record** of the network layout and the devices on it, so the next check is faster.

Never open the network wider than it was, or share its password, without a person saying so.
