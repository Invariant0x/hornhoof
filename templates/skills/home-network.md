1. **Read the state**: within existing access, read which devices are online, signal strength by room, the internet connection, firmware versions, and any outage or slowdown. Device metadata needs no action approval; it gives no right to camera streams, microphone content or recordings.
2. **Find the weak spots**: a room with poor coverage, an old firmware, a channel that clashes with neighbours, a device nobody recognises on the network.
3. **Tune the network**. Use the rules provided with the assignment or available in the home's notebook. Record the existing setting, make the permitted change and keep a way to restore it. Stop after findings on a read-only assignment.
4. **After a change**, check that the home is back to normal and say what changed.
5. **Keep a short record** of the network layout and the devices on it, so the next check is faster.

Keep the layout, device list and findings private to the home. Use the assigned access and sharing rules. Camera and microphone content needs a separate assignment and existing access. Return the findings and any change to the head.
