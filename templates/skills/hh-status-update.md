When asked for status, or when the department finishes or gets stuck on something that matters, write:

- **Done**: what the department finished since the last update, with links or names.
- **In progress**: what runs now, who has it, when it should end.
- **Blocked or at risk**: what stops progress and who can unblock it. Put the one thing you need from the CEO first.
- **Decisions made**: choices you took on the company's behalf and what each costs if wrong.
- **Next**: what starts next.

Keep it under 12 lines, in plain words, with numbers where they exist. Bad news goes first and early; a delay reported the day it is known is cheap, the same delay found at the deadline is not. Never report work as done that a gate has not passed.
