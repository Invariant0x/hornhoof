Choose a forecast horizon and reporting cadence that expose the next cash decisions. Use dated account balances, expected receipts and obligations from the source records. State the timing, currency conversions and uncertain receipts. Show closing cash at useful intervals and flag when it falls below the minimum set by the people responsible for the funds.

Choose a representative period for burn and runway and show how the result changes if receipts or expenses move. Describe what changed since the previous forecast and give the responsible Finance people concrete actions with their amounts and dates. Identify the material figures and sources for the required release check.
