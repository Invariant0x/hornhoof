1. **Goal**: one measurable result with a date, such as "200 sign-ups from developers by the end of the turn". If it cannot be measured, ask before planning.
2. **Audience**: which segment of the product marketing context, and the one message it should take away.
3. **Channels**: two or three where that audience already is: the website and search, social networks, communities, email, partners. Say why each, and drop the rest.
4. **Pieces**: everything to make, each with a member, a due date and the channel it serves. Reuse: cut one core piece, such as an article or a launch page, into posts and emails.
5. **Launches**: for a product or feature launch, add the steps before (page ready, proof collected, sales briefed through the orchestrator), on the day (the order of announcements across channels) and after (follow-up posts, questions answered, results read).
6. **Approval time**: every public piece passes mkt-publish-approval; plan a day for it.
7. **Budget**: anything paid needs the CEO's approval, and a finance check if the company has a finance department.
8. **Measures**: the number to watch per channel, where it is read, and when you report it.

Show the plan to the CEO through the orchestrator before work starts if it costs money or speaks for the company in a new way.
