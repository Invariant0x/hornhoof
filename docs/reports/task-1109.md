## Root causes and implementation

The synthetic proc sample has a hook holder 112 under orphan shell 111, with
ppid 1, while the original pane leader 100 still exists. Its session 100 and
tty 34817 are unchanged. The original ancestry lookup reaches 112, 111 and 1,
never 100, and rejects it. Both the hinted and legacy socket-holder searches
now also verify the pane's kernel terminal session. A session with another
leader remains foreign even when its tty number matches. The accepted socket
inode and its actual holder are still required. No environment variable or
caller-supplied identity grants access.

The tmux error sample reproduces the other rejection path: host.panes changed
a failed command into an empty list, indistinguishable from a foreign caller.
The bridge then cached false for that connection. A pane lookup now retries
once immediately; a second failure produces HTTP 502 and removes the failed
ownership lookup from the connection cache. Recovery can recheck the same
connection. This also gives one-shot status hooks a retry inside the node.

These are proven causes in the synthetic boundary samples, not attribution
of individual production events. The reported 258 rejections and about 760
tool events do not identify which cause produced which event, and are not a
matched numerator and denominator for a hook rejection rate. Original a-host
and 0x.local process and tmux recordings were not supplied.

The server queries program.view on the current turn's node. It requires the
same job and program instance, a successful fresh view, idle state, empty
input, no tool, question or undelivered words, and an unchanged screen and
activity marker across at least two minutes of fresh observations. A failed
view, instance change, new activity, draft or gap over two minutes resets the
observation. It closes the turn even with no queued successor. The usual
hook-driven recovery and its Operations escalation remain in place.

The independent node signal is deliberately narrow: Claude's completed
duration line with `done` immediately above its current empty prompt, with
no pending call, question, retry, restart or typing. An empty prompt during
work or a completion marker in older history gives no idle proof.

## Concerns and limits

- The proc and tmux fixtures are synthetic, not incident recordings. Their
  origin is recorded in the fixture JSON itself. Operations confirmed via
  the orchestrator on task 1109 that the original a-host and 0x.local logs
  for 10:30 to 13:00 on November 16 were lost when containers were recreated
  and cannot be recovered. They reported one sanitized 11:30 diagnostic
  excerpt with 48 ownership rejection lines, without PIDs, process trees or
  tmux errors. That count establishes neither reproduced cause; the excerpt
  itself was not yet available to Development when this report was updated.
- Independent screen recovery currently recognizes Claude only. Codex,
  Kimi and Antigravity retain their hook/log-based completion. If those
  channels lose an end and keep reporting work, this patch supplies no
  independent screen close for them. The head accepted this limit and assigned
  follow-up task 1123. Unknown or cropped Claude layouts also stay open.
- The node and server fixtures exercise our process-table parsing, HTTP
  boundary and scheduler. They do not run or test tmux or agent programs.
- No release, shared-branch integration or whole-suite run is performed here.
